Privacy Policy

HowSoon

Effective date: 2026-11-23

At a glance

  • No sign-up, no login, no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We run no servers. Your dates (their names, dates and settings), the photos you add to them and your settings are stored on your device. Date names and photos are not sent to us.
  • Reminders are scheduled and shown by the app on your device. They do not come from a server.
  • Only three Google services send data off the device:
    • the banner ad at the bottom of the app screens (Google AdMob). There are no ads on the home screen widgets or in reminders.
    • usage statistics we use to improve the app (Google Analytics for Firebase)
    • crash reports when the app stops working (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • You add photos with Android's photo picker. The app receives only the photos you choose and does not ask for permission to your photos.
  • When you export, the app makes one file with your dates and photos and either hands it to your device's share sheet or saves it in a folder you choose. You choose where it goes; it is not sent to us (section 6).
  • After a celebration for a day or a milestone, the app may ask Google Play once to show its review window. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play without passing through the app and is published like any other review; the app does not receive it (section 6).
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing ads.

5. Consent — giving, changing and withdrawing it

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent window appears the first time you open the app. We ask through a Google-certified consent management tool (UMP), and your choices apply to both ads and usage statistics.

  • If you consent, you may see personalized ads based on your interests.
  • If you do not consent, you can still use the whole app. Ads will be non-personalized. Even then, minimal data (IP address, approximate location, device info, advertising identifiers, etc.) is processed to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears and prevent fraud. Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is processed.

6. Exporting, importing and links

Export. When you tap Export in Settings, the app makes one file (named like HowSoon-2026-01-31.json) with your dates and photos. If you choose Share, it passes the file to your device's share sheet; if you choose Save to device, it saves the file in the folder you pick in your device's save dialog. The file holds the names of your dates, the dates, your settings for each date and your photos, so it can include text you typed yourself and pictures of people. When you share, the file is made in the app's temporary storage on your device; when you save, it is written only where you choose. You choose where it goes; the app itself does not send it anywhere, and we do not receive it. Whoever gets the file can read it, so please check it before you send.

Import. When you tap Import and pick a file, the app reads it on your device, shows how many dates and photos it holds, and after you confirm, merges them into your dates. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Photos and files. You pick photos and files with Android's own picker and save dialogs, so the app receives only what you choose. This app does not ask for storage or photo permissions.

Rating and other apps. Rate on Google Play and More apps in About open Google Play. Links to the apps listed under More apps end with utm_source=other_games&utm_campaign=howsoon, a mark showing they came from HowSoon's About screen. This mark reaches Google Play when the link is opened, and contains nothing that identifies a person. We only see how many visits came this way, as totals in Google Play Console. Rate on Google Play and See all apps carry no such mark. After a celebration, the app may ask Google Play once to show its review window. Google Play decides whether the window appears. Any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play without passing through the app and is published like any other review; the app does not receive it (Google Privacy Policy).

8. International transfers

Usage statistics, crash reports and ad data are processed on Google's servers, including outside Korea and the EEA (for example in the United States). Google states that it uses legally recognized transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

9. How long data is kept

  • Usage statistics — user-level data is deleted after 2 months.
  • Crash reports — deleted after 90 days.
  • On your device — until you uninstall the app (data in your device backup follows Google's backup policy). A date stays until you delete it. A deleted date is removed 30 days later, when you next open the app, or at once if you choose Delete for good or Empty deleted dates. Photos that no date uses any more are removed after a day. Of the daily copies, the latest 3 days are kept; older ones are replaced.
  • Ads — according to Google's retention policies; see Google's documents above.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the following rights under the GDPR (Articles 15–22):

  • access, rectification and erasure
  • restriction of and objection to processing
  • data portability
  • withdrawing consent at any time (processing before withdrawal remains lawful)
  • lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority

Where to send a request. We do not know your name or contact details, so we cannot find a specific person's records in the usage statistics. When you uninstall the app, its app instance ID is removed, so later data cannot be linked to earlier data, and the remaining data is deleted after the retention periods above. Requests about ad data must go to Google, which actually holds it (see the Google Privacy Policy). If you are not sure what to ask or where, write to hanworld@willee.net and we will help as best we can.

Complaints. EEA residents may complain to the data protection authority in their country, and UK residents to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In Korea, you can report to the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, dial 118).

11. Children

This app is not directed at children. We list it on Google Play for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are the parent or guardian of a child under 16 (13 to 16 depending on the member state) and are concerned about their use of this app, please contact hanworld@willee.net.
