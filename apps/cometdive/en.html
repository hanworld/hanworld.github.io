Cometdive

Space shooter · Classic · Daily

Android (Coming Soon)

Cometdive is an offline arcade space shooter you play with one hand. Drag left or right anywhere on the screen to steer your ship; it fires on its own. Enemies fly in as formations and dive at you. Catch the divers to fill the comet gauge, then become the comet and pierce straight through the formation.

Screenshots

Two Ways to Play

ClassicEight sectors make a zone, and from sector 9 the sectors keep coming with no end. Every zone ends with a boss, the score multiplier climbs with each zone up to ×3, and your record is the highest sector you reach
DailyOne sector a day. Players who meet the same boss that day get the same waves and card choices, and everyone flies the same ship with no hangar upgrades. Finish it to keep your streak

Key Features

  • Comet Dive Diving enemies are worth double and fill the comet gauge faster than enemies holding formation. When the gauge is full, flick up fast, or tap the control strip along the bottom of the screen, and your ship surges up the screen as a comet. Choose your moment, because the gauge has to fill again.
  • One-handed controls Drag left or right anywhere on the screen to steer your ship; it fires on its own. You can also drag on the control strip along the bottom, so your finger never covers the ship. A short tutorial teaches the controls. Skip it, or replay it from Settings.
  • A card after every wave After every wave you pick one of three cards: multi-shot, spread shot, rapid fire, piercing shots, homing missiles, wing drones, shields, side guns, chain arcs, a pulse laser and more. Cards start over every sector, so roguelike-style choices build a different ship each time.
  • Enemies and bosses Sixteen kinds of enemy come in tougher tiers, with elite versions, drifting rocks, mines, beams and gravity wells in the mix. Six bosses (Titan, Nebula, Prism, Star Furnace, Orrery and Ion Spire) each return in a second form. Enemies and bosses show a warning before their heavy attacks, so a hit is something you can learn to avoid.
  • Hangar Earn cores by playing and spend them in the Hangar on 35 upgrade steps across five branches: Main gun, Comet, Engine, Hull and Tactics. As the upgrades add up, your ship changes through ten forms, MK I to MK X, and you can look back at any form you have reached. Tactics lets you pick a card before wave 1 and re-roll your card choices.
  • Codex The Codex lists 109 entries: enemies, elites, bosses, obstacles, cards and enemy shots. Ones you have not met yet appear as silhouettes, and it fills in as you play.
  • Sound and effects Settings cover game music, menu music, music and sound effect volume, screen shake and Reduce effects.
  • Offline play Everything works offline, Daily included. There is no account to make, and your records stay on your device.
  • Multilingual English, Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), following your device language or your choice in Settings.
  • Ads and purchases There are no ads or purchases during play. A banner sits under the home, sector select and records screens, and a full-screen ad can appear after a result screen, only after about 20 minutes of play and no more than once every 15 minutes. Nothing in the app costs money to buy.
