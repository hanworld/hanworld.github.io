Board Games

Board Games with AI & Local 2-Player

Android (Coming Soon)

Board Games brings seven classic board games together in one app: Gomoku, Othello, Checkers, Backgammon, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes, and Tic-Tac-Toe. Play the AI on your own, or a friend on one device. No sign-up, no login — and it works without an internet connection.

The Games

GomokuFirst to five in a row wins
OthelloFlip and be flipped, most wins
CheckersJump, capture, and be crowned
BackgammonBear off all fifteen to win
Connect FourDrop four in a row to win
Dots and BoxesClaim the most boxes to win
Tic-Tac-ToeFirst to three in a row wins

Key Features

  • Seven Games Gomoku, Othello, Checkers, Backgammon, Connect Four, Dots and Boxes and Tic-Tac-Toe in one app.
  • Play vs AI Choose Easy, Normal or Hard difficulty and who moves first — play on your own anytime.
  • Local 2-Player Sit down with a friend and take turns on one device.
  • Works Offline The games run without an internet connection. The internet is only used to fetch the banner ad.
  • Undo & Restart Take back a move you regret, or restart a fresh game with the same settings.
  • Multilingual Korean, English, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified), following your device language.
  • Free Free with banner ads, no sign-up, and minimal data collection.
