TarotYo

Tarot Card Reading & AI Prompt Generator

Android (Coming Soon)

TarotYo is an app that draws cards from the 78 tarot cards and turns the result into a prompt (a question to ask) that an AI such as ChatGPT can interpret. The app does not interpret the cards itself — ChatGPT (or another AI) helps with the interpretation. It is content meant for fun, emotional comfort, and organizing your thoughts, not for giving fixed answers.

Screenshots

Key Features

  • Draw Tarot Cards Draw cards using five spreads: Three Card, Two Choices, Situation Advice, Relationship, and Celtic Cross.
  • AI Prompt Generation Create a prompt containing the meanings of your cards and their positions, then get an interpretation from an AI such as ChatGPT.
  • Browse Cards Freely browse all 78 cards: 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana.
  • Card Back Design Choose the card back design shown when you draw cards.
  • Multilingual Support Available in Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese.
  • Free to Use Provided free of charge with banner ads, with no sign-up and minimal data collection.
