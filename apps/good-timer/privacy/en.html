Privacy Policy

Good Timer

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Willee Project ("Developer") is committed to protecting the privacy of Good Timer ("App") users. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

The App does not use any analytics tools or tracking SDKs. However, the following services may automatically collect certain information:

Advertising (Android only)

  • The App serves ads through Google AdMob.
  • AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers (GAID) and ad interaction data.
  • For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

1.2 Information You Provide

Google Account Information (when using Cloud Sync)

  • When you sign in with Google for Drive sync, the App accesses your email address and display name.
  • The App only requests the drive.file scope, which limits access to files created by the App.

In-App Purchases (Android only)

  • Purchases are processed through Google Play. The Developer does not have access to payment details (e.g., card numbers).

1.3 Information Stored on Your Device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted externally (unless Cloud Sync is enabled):

  • Timer presets (names, phase configurations, rounds)
  • Timer session history (start/end times, completed rounds)
  • Stopwatch records (labels, elapsed time, lap records)
  • App settings (theme, language, sound/vibration preferences)

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used solely for the following purposes:

4. Data Retention and Deletion

4.1 On-Device Data

  • You can delete data directly within the App.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all local data.

4.2 Cloud Data (Google Drive)

  • Data is stored in your personal Google Drive.
  • You can delete cloud data using "Reset Cloud" in App settings.
  • Signing out of Google blocks the App's access to your Drive.
  • Deleted records are permanently removed from the cloud 30 days after deletion during sync.

4.3 Advertising Data

  • Data collected by Google AdMob is managed according to Google's policies.
  • You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • Access: View all stored data within the App.
  • Deletion: Delete records within the App or uninstall the App entirely.
  • Opt out of Sync: Choose not to use Google Drive sync.
  • Limit Ads: Restrict ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Manage Purchases: Manage subscriptions and payments through Google Play.

6. Additional Rights for Users in the European Economic Area (EEA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users residing in the EEA have the following additional rights:

6.1 Legal Basis for Processing

Processing ActivityLegal Basis
Core App features (Timer/Stopwatch)Performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
Cloud syncConsent (Art. 6(1)(a))
Ad deliveryLegitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
In-app purchasesPerformance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b))

6.2 Additional Rights

  • Right of Access: You may request a copy of your personal data being processed.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You may request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: You may request restriction of processing under certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may receive your data in a structured format. The App's backup feature allows you to export your data in JSON format.
  • Right to Object: You may object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing. For cloud sync, you can withdraw by signing out of your Google account.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You may file a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence.

6.3 International Data Transfers

When using Cloud Sync, your data may be transferred to servers outside the EEA through your Google Drive. These transfers are protected under Google's Data Processing Terms and Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will promptly delete it.

8. Security

The Developer takes the following measures to protect your information:

  • All network communication uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Google Drive API access uses OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • The App requests only minimal permissions (only vibration permission is explicitly requested).

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as needed. If significant changes are made, we will notify you through an App update. The updated policy takes effect from the date it is published in the App.
