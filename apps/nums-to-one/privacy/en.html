Privacy Policy

Nums to One

Effective date: 2026-10-05

At a glance

  • No sign-up, no login, no account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We run no servers. Your game records and settings are stored on your device.
  • Only three Google services send data off the device:
    • the banner ad at the bottom of the screen (Google AdMob)
    • usage statistics we use to improve the app (Google Analytics for Firebase)
    • crash reports when the app stops working (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing ads.

2. What is stored on your device

WhatDetails
Game recordsIDs and solve times of the puzzles you solved, success rate by tier, skill rating per target, Rush best score and games played, Daily results, streak and the days you finished, the Classic target you last chose on the home screen, whether you finished the first-run guide

This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Device backup. If you have turned on your device's Google backup (Android Auto Backup), the records and settings above are included in that backup, so they come back when you switch devices or reinstall the app. The backup is stored in your Google account; we cannot see it. You can turn it off in the backup menu of your Android settings.

Separately, the ad SDK and Firebase store consent records (such as your choice in the EEA) and the identifiers listed below on your device.

3. Usage statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

We receive only what we need to improve the app. There is no personal information such as your name or email. We see the data below as statistics in the Firebase console.

Usage statistics — Google Analytics for Firebase

WhatExamplesWhy
App instance IDA random ID created when the app is installedCounting whether the same device comes back the next day (retention)

  • Data at the level of individual users is deleted automatically after 2 months (Google Analytics data retention setting). After that, only aggregate numbers that cannot be linked to anyone remain.

Google processes this data on our behalf. For details, see Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Linked to ads

Usage statistics are linked to the ads in section 4 (Google AdMob), so we can see ad revenue together with usage metrics (number of users, return visits, etc.). Google may use the linked usage statistics to improve AdMob features and the app's ad revenue. The advertising ID is not used here either.

5. Consent — giving, changing and withdrawing it

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent window appears the first time you open the app. We ask through a Google-certified consent management tool (UMP), and your choices apply to both ads and usage statistics.

  • If you consent, you may see personalized ads based on your interests.
  • If you do not consent, you can still use the whole app. Ads will be non-personalized. Even then, minimal data (IP address, approximate location, device info, advertising identifiers, etc.) is processed to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears and prevent fraud. Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is processed.

If you change your mind

On your deviceAndroid settings → Privacy → Ads → reset or delete your advertising ID
To stop entirelyUninstalling the app deletes the records and the app instance ID on your device

6. Sharing

When you tap Share on a result screen, the result text (for example Nums to One #42 🔥7) is passed to your device's share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app itself does not send it anywhere.

8. International transfers

Usage statistics, crash reports and ad data are processed on Google's servers, including outside Korea and the EEA (for example in the United States). Google states that it uses legally recognized transfer mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).

9. How long data is kept

  • Usage statistics — user-level data is deleted after 2 months.
  • Crash reports — deleted after 90 days.
  • On your device — until you uninstall the app (data in your device backup follows Google's backup policy).
  • Ads — according to Google's retention policies; see Google's documents above.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the following rights under the GDPR (Articles 15–22):

  • access, rectification and erasure
  • restriction of and objection to processing
  • data portability
  • withdrawing consent at any time (processing before withdrawal remains lawful)
  • lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority

Where to send a request. We do not know your name or contact details, so we cannot find a specific person's records in the usage statistics. When you uninstall the app, its app instance ID is removed, so later data cannot be linked to earlier data, and the remaining data is deleted after the retention periods above. Requests about ad data must go to Google, which actually holds it (see the Google Privacy Policy). If you are not sure what to ask or where, write to hanworld@willee.net and we will help as best we can.

Complaints. EEA residents may complain to the data protection authority in their country, and UK residents to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In Korea, you can report to the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, dial 118).

11. Children

This app is not directed at children. It is listed on Google Play for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are the parent or guardian of a child under 16 (13 to 16 depending on the member state) and are concerned about their use of this app, please contact hanworld@willee.net.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post it on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes, we will also let you know through an app update notice.
