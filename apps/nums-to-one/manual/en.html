A puzzle where you merge number cards with + − × ÷ to make one target number. The default target is 24.

  • No sign-up, no login. Open the app and the first puzzle is right there.
  • The game works offline. The internet is used only for the banner ad and usage statistics.
  • The language follows your device. English, Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) are supported, and you can also pick one in Settings.

1. Rules

Use all four cards to make the target. Pick two cards and one operator to merge them into a single card. Merge three times; if the last card is the target, you've solved it.

OperatorRule
+ plusAdds the two numbers
− minusSubtracts the smaller number from the larger one, whatever order you tap them in
× timesMultiplies the two numbers
÷ divideDivides the larger number by the smaller one, only when it divides evenly. 0 divided with any other number gives 0. 0 and 0 cannot be divided

Results are always whole numbers of 0 or more — never negative numbers or fractions.

If the last card is not the target, the cards shake and go back to the starting four. The puzzle stays the same, so just try again.

2. Controls

Tap card → operator → card. The result appears where the second card was.

  • Card → card → operator and operator → card → card work too.
  • Tap a selected card again to put it down. Tap the selected operator again to cancel it.
  • Each merge stays in the step log next to the target card (for example 7 ÷ 7 = 1). The card and operator you're picking show there too, and when you solve, the whole expression appears on one line.

The row of buttons at the bottom:

ButtonWhat it does
UndoUndoes the last merge
RestartGoes back to the starting four cards
HintHelps one step more each time you tap it (see below)
SkipMoves on to the next puzzle
HintWhat it shows
Step 1The two cards to merge first light up
Step 2The operator for those two cards lights up too
Step 3Shows the whole answer. This counts as skipped, not solved

The dots under the Hint button show how many steps you've used. After step 2 the button reads Show answer. The move that steps 1 and 2 point to also appears faintly in the step log.

If the target can no longer be made from your current cards, Undo glows gold instead of a hint, and no hint step is used.

3. Modes

Classic — no timer

  • On the home screen, choose a target of 10, 24 or 36 and tap Play. Your choice is remembered. 36 unlocks after you solve 30 puzzles of 24.
  • Puzzle tiers (1–5 stars) follow your skill. Solving quickly without hints brings harder puzzles; using hints or skipping makes them easier.
  • The top bar shows the puzzle's tier, how many you've solved this session and your streak. The streak ends when you skip or reveal the answer (solving with hints keeps it going).

Rush — 60 seconds

  • Solve as many as you can in 60 seconds. Puzzles start easy and get harder.
  • A 3 · 2 · 1 countdown comes first. The clock doesn't run during it.
  • Score = (tier × 100 + speed bonus) × combo multiplier
    • Speed bonus: 10 points for every second under 10 seconds
    • Combo multiplier: rises by 0.1 with each puzzle in a row, up to ×2
  • Skipping costs 2 seconds and ends your combo. There are no hints in Rush.
  • The clock stops while the success animation plays, and when you leave the app.
  • In the last 10 seconds the timer turns red and ticks.

Daily — today's three puzzles

  • The same three puzzles every day, the same for everyone in the world (by your device's date).
  • No time limit; your total time for the three is recorded and shown at the top as you play. You can use hints, but they show in your result.
  • You can play once a day. If you leave midway, that attempt is dropped and you start over. After you finish, tapping Daily opens your result.
  • Result marks: a check (green) solved without hints, a bulb (yellow) solved with hints, an X (gray) skipped or answer revealed. The shared text uses 🟩🟨⬛.
  • Each day you finish keeps your streak going. The app shows it with a calendar check; the shared text uses 🔥. Tap Share to send your result, for example:
Nums to One #42  🔥7
🟩🟩🟨  1:42

The result screen shows how long until the next Daily. A new Daily opens at midnight on your device's clock.

#42 on the result card and in the shared text is the Daily number. Launch day was #1 and it goes up by one each day, so the same number means the same day's puzzles. The home and Daily screens show today's date instead.

4. Records

Tap the bar chart button at the top right of the home screen. The top shows puzzles solved, average solve time, your Rush best score with games played, and your Daily streak. Below that:

  • Classic: your skill for targets 10, 24 and 36 (1–5 stars) and how many different puzzles you've solved out of the total
  • Success rate by tier: a bar for each tier
  • Daily: a calendar of the last 5 weeks. Finished days are green; today has a gold outline.

5. Settings

Tap the gear at the top right of the home screen. During a game, the slider button at the top right changes sound, vibration and effects without leaving the puzzle.

ItemWhat it does
Sound effectsTurns card, operator and success sounds on or off
VibrationTurns vibration on or off when you pick or merge cards
Reduce effectsTurns off debris, shaking and flashing
LanguageFollows your device or lets you choose
Privacy optionsReopens ad consent. Shown only in regions where consent is required (such as Europe)
AboutVersion, this guide, privacy policy, open-source licenses

6. Ads and your records

  • A single banner ad sits at the very bottom of the screen. During Rush and the first-run guide the ad is removed and only its space is kept.
  • Your records and settings are stored on your device. If your device's Google backup is on, they are included in it, so you can carry on after switching devices. Uninstalling the app deletes the records on your device.
  • For details, see the privacy policy.
