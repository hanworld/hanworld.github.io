Tenmates

Every number has a mate.

Tenmates

Match 10 number game · Classic · Rush · Daily

Android (Google Play)

Tenmates is a number puzzle: find pairs that are the same or add up to 10, clear them, and empty the board. Every number has a mate. Tap a number, then its mate. Pairs can touch in any direction, sit far apart with only cleared cells between them, or even wrap from the end of one row to the start of the next. No pairs left? Add the remaining numbers to the end of the board and keep going.

Screenshots

Three Ways to Play

ClassicNo timer. Clear boards to unlock bigger ones, from 5×4 to 9×12
Rush90 seconds. Every board you empty adds time, and the next one grows
DailyOne new board a day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak and share

Key Features

  • Trios Three numbers in a line that add up to 10, like 2, 3 and 5, clear all at once. Spot them to clear the board faster.
  • Every board can be emptied Each board is checked by a solver before it ships.
  • Hints that think ahead A hint shows a pair on a path that can still empty the board, not just any pair you could clear. Hints and undos are free and unlimited in Classic and Daily.
  • Large numbers An option that draws the numbers on the board bigger, for easy reading.
  • Satisfying feel Pair animations, sounds and vibration. Turn off any effect you don't want, or reduce them all.
  • Offline play No sign-up, no login. The game works without an internet connection.
  • Multilingual English, Korean, Japanese and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), following your device language or your choice in Settings.
  • Free to play Free with one small banner ad at the bottom, removed while you play Rush.
