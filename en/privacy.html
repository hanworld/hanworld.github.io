Privacy Policy

Willee Project

Effective Date: November 13, 2026

Willee Project (hereinafter "Company") values the privacy of users and complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the willee.net website and to email inquiries. Personal information handled in our apps is not covered by this policy; it is governed by each app's own privacy policy (see Section 4).

2. Website

  • There is no sign-up, login, or input form. We do not use cookies or analytics or advertising tools. The Company does not collect personal information through this website.
  • If you choose the light or dark theme with the button at the top of the page, only that choice (light or dark) is saved in your browser's storage (localStorage). It is not sent to the Company, and it is removed when you clear this site's data in your browser.
  • This website runs on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub's documentation, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. The Company does not receive these logs; their retention and handling are governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub stores and processes personal data in several countries, including the United States.
  • If you follow a link to another site, such as YouTube, our blog, or Google Play, that site's privacy policy applies.

3. Email Inquiries

This section applies to inquiries sent to hanworld@willee.net, including those you send after seeing the address in an app or on Google Play.

  • Information received: your email address and the content of your inquiry, including anything you write or attach in the email
  • Purpose: to reply to and handle your inquiry
  • Retention: kept for 3 years from the date we finish replying, then destroyed without delay. However, if retention is required by relevant laws, the information will be kept for the specified period.
  • Storage: inquiry emails are stored in the email service the Company uses.
  • Provision to third parties: not provided to third parties without user consent. However, exceptions may apply when required by law.
  • Access, correction, and deletion: send a request to the email address in Section 6, and we will handle it without delay, even during the retention period (except for information that must be kept by law).

4. Personal Information in Apps

The personal information handled in each app, and the advertising and analytics tools it uses, are described in that app's privacy policy. Choose an app from the App Services list on the home page to reach its policy.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will post it on this page with a new effective date.

6. Privacy Officer

Please send questions and requests about personal information to the email address below. In Korea, you can report to the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, dial 118). EEA residents may complain to the data protection authority in their country, and UK residents to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
